Full Fridge

Improving the shared fridge experience and storage organisation

A project developed around the Student Kitchens at Halmstad University, looking at how students use the shared fridges and how the space could be organised differently.

Research Observation Physical Prototyping Interaction
Explore project ↓

Project Context

This project started with a broad exploration of shared spaces at Halmstad University. Our group decided to focus on the Student Kitchen and understand how students experience and use this shared environment.

Understanding the Experience

We explored the Student Kitchen through interviews, observations and mapping activities. As the project developed, the shared fridge became our main area of focus.

From Research to Ideas

Different directions were explored around fridge use, organisation and the experience of sharing the space.

Prototyping

The project moved towards a combination of private compartments and shared organisation using organisers inside the fridge.
